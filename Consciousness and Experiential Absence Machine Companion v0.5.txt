STRUCTURAL FLOW — CONSCIOUSNESS FORMAL METHODS / MACHINE COMPANION
Consciousness and Experiential Absence: Formal Object, Admission Contract, Machine Stop, and Universal-Kernel Interface
ABSTRACT
This companion exposes the formal and machine-auditable backbone of the paper Consciousness and Experiential Absence: An Evidentiary Contract and Staged Experimental Program without requiring the primary paper to carry the full Structural Flow or Lean development. The candidate object is C3R: Consciousness as current experiential standing at bounded bearer / fixed-passage scope. The formal package keeps standing capacity for Consciousness, current experiential mode, conscious content, ordinary carried content, behavioral or physiological support, and retrospective report distinct. It then places empirical cases under an Experiential Admission Contract with three lawful evidentiary standings: POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, and INDETERMINATE.
The current encoded science ledger contains a POSITIVE experiential anchor and no admitted NEGATIVE experiential anchor. NREM complete-unconsciousness candidates, meditative cessation, and propofol/xenon no-experience candidates remain INDETERMINATE at the frozen evidentiary standard. Lean proves the consequences of that frozen ledger: empirical placement is not ready, and no machine closure packet of the encoded C3R form can be inhabited without an admitted NEGATIVE anchor. A separate Consciousness × Universal Kernel v1.0 adapter places the missing NEGATIVE anchor as an OPEN required domain-entry burden while leaving the biological/physical realization mechanism OPEN as a downstream-domain burden. With no required burden encoded as violated, the public Universal Translation Contract returns NotYetAdjudicable rather than NoLawfulTranslation or StructuralConforms.
The machine result is therefore a disciplined stop, not empirical validation. It does not prove that C3R is the final definition of Consciousness, that experiential absence exists, that any present candidate passage is NEGATIVE, that the admission contract is empirically complete, or that any neural mechanism causes Consciousness. The missing NEGATIVE anchor is carried forward as a staged experimental burden: Stage A attempts to validate the negative instrument against independently known-positive experience under memory/access challenge; if that gate passes, Stage B applies the validated instrument to prospectively bounded extended-cessation target passages; Stage C applies the frozen adjudication rule to locked evidence. No Universal Kernel repair is required at the present checkpoint because the unresolved empirical and realization burdens are lawfully located as domain-owned burdens rather than missing universal structure.
1. PURPOSE AND CLAIM BOUNDARY
The primary paper carries the scientific problem, current evidence gap, and staged experimental program. This companion carries the formal derivation, evidentiary admission architecture, machine encoding, machine stop, public Universal Kernel interface, and reproducibility burden.
The companion does not rederive the full Structural Flow TOE. It uses only the structure needed to identify C3R, distinguish it from neighboring concepts, state the evidentiary contract under which it can be tested, and show why the present evidence produces a machine stop rather than empirical closure. The formal scope is deliberately narrower than a general theory of Consciousness. Object closure at that scope is distinct from a later question of universal-kernel residency or broader explanatory reach.
2. THE C3R OBJECT 
C3R names Consciousness as current experiential standing. At the scope used here, Consciousness is the passage-specific held mode of a bounded consciousness-capable system in which experiential standing is currently instantiated for the bearer.
The object is deliberately narrow. It asks whether experience is present for this bearer during this passage without defining Consciousness by a particular content, report channel, behavioral output, neural mechanism, complexity measure, or memory trace. Its job is to preserve that passage-level distinction when neighboring variables change.
The corresponding failure case is equally narrow:
STANDING CAPACITY FOR CONSCIOUSNESS REMAINS
while
CURRENT EXPERIENTIAL STANDING CLOSES.
Without this distinction, absence of current experiential standing can collapse into loss of capacity, content, report, responsiveness, memory, or mechanism. C3R therefore concerns a bounded system at a fixed passage and does not require universal status for object closure.
Its present empirical standing is NOT YET ADJUDICABLE because no lawful NEGATIVE experiential anchor has yet cleared the admission contract.
C3R does not specify a universal biological substrate, neural mechanism, causal theory, phenomenal content taxonomy, threshold of complexity, report architecture, or condition under which a system first becomes consciousness-capable. It also does not claim that Consciousness itself belongs in the public Universal Kernel.
3. DISTINCTIONS THE FORMALIZATION PRESERVES
The derivation depends on preserving three separations.
3.1 Capacity for Consciousness is not current Consciousness
The empirical interface contains a distinct capacityForMode predicate. The machine architecture therefore permits a case in which the bearer remains consciousness-capable while the selected mode shape fails. This is the exact granularity required by the C3R failure discriminant. Evidence that a carrier remains capable of Consciousness is not, by itself, evidence that the carrier is currently in the Consciousness mode.
3.2 Current Consciousness is not a particular conscious content
The formal interface separately represents modeExperiential and contentExperiential. Positive experiential content implies positive experiential mode under the domain interface, but the reverse identity is not installed. A bearer may therefore retain current experiential standing while particular content changes, becomes unavailable, or is not identified.
3.3 Carried content is not automatically conscious content
The empirical interface also contains ordinary contentCarried independently of contentExperiential. Carrying a distinction does not make that distinction conscious by definition. This blocks the machine from silently promoting stored, processed, globally available, recurrent, integrated, or behaviorally usable content into experiential content without a domain-owned warrant.
4. WHY NEIGHBORING STRUCTURES DO NOT REPLACE C3R
The present claim is not that Consciousness is independent of every neighboring structure in realization. The narrower claim is that none of the competent neighbors below performs C3R's exact scoped job: adjudicating whether experiential standing is currently instantiated for a pinned bearer during a pinned passage.
Persistence retains an interaction-produced difference beyond its production. Retained availability does not decide whether the bearer is currently experiencing.
Boundary discriminates retained carry through a maintained relational cut. Discrimination can be present without deciding current experiential standing.
Cascade carries an update into a distinct further interaction. Inheritance by a later interaction is neither necessary nor sufficient, by definition, for current experience in the original passage.
Authorization incorporates an inherited update into the carrier-supported condition. Incorporation is not current experiential standing.
Recognition preserves authorized carried content as comparably the same binding constraint across locally variable descriptions or uptake surfaces. Recognition concerns sameness across variation, not whether experience is currently instantiated.
Coherence holds already-recognized local structure jointly as one system or order with shared invariants. System-level joint hold does not answer the passage-specific experiential question by itself.
Density concerns non-empty common interaction support among plural incorporated distinctions. Common support can vary while the C3R question remains independently live.
Memory is retained prior difference with standing later answerability. Memory may provide evidence about a past experiential passage, but retention and later answerability are not the same object as current experiential standing.
Intelligence is maintenance capacity: a bounded system's capacity to sustain coherence through executable and communicable maintenance under entropy. Maintenance capacity does not answer whether experience is currently instantiated.
Agency is the standing capacity of a bounded persistent system, under imperfect knowledge, to select among structurally reachable actions. Standing selection capacity is not current experiential standing.
Free Will is the passage-specific mode of Agency in which effective local selectability remains operative across plural reachable continuations long enough to route which continuation begins to carry. This is another passage-specific mode, but its job is local selectability, not experiential standing.
The non-absorption claim therefore remains scoped and functional. These neighbors may participate in, support, condition, correlate with, or be required by particular realizations of Consciousness. None is promoted into C3R merely because such a relation may exist.
5. WHY COMMON PROXIES DO NOT DEFINE CURRENT EXPERIENCE
Report, responsiveness, behavior, memory, attention, recurrence, integration, complexity, global availability, self-monitoring, self-model, metacognition, wakefulness, and rich content may all be scientifically informative. None, however, receives authority to define current experiential standing by default.
The formal reason is simple: the Consciousness contract source keeps empirical support conditions, ordinary carried content, standing capacity, and experiential predicates in different fields. A candidate support or correlate can be registered without becoming the object. The admission contract additionally blocks several common null patterns from becoming NEGATIVE merely because the expected positive signal is missing.
6. EXPERIENTIAL ADMISSION CONTRACT
6.1 Three evidentiary outcomes
For an eligible pinned case, the empirical interface permits:
POSITIVE — admissible evidence warrants experiential standing in the target passage.
NEGATIVE — admissible evidence warrants absence of experiential standing in the target passage.
INDETERMINATE — the available evidence does not lawfully settle the passage under the contract.
These are evidentiary standings. They are not ordinary-language state names and are not ontological kinds.
6.2 Identifying the bearer and passage
A POSITIVE or NEGATIVE admission requires a pinned carrier, a pinned passage, and a declared mode target. A general state label, broad condition, or retrospective category is not sufficient if the evidence cannot be attributed to the passage being classified.
6.3 Independence from the candidate being tested
The state label, candidate mechanism, physiological theory, or desired Consciousness interpretation cannot provide its own experiential classification. If candidate independence fails, the contract routes the case to INDETERMINATE.
6.4 Timing
The evidentiary warrant must be attributable to the target passage. Inadequate temporal anchoring routes the case to INDETERMINATE.
6.5 Retrospective memory and access
If the mode claim is retrospective, unresolved memory confounding blocks NEGATIVE. No later recall or no retrievable content cannot by itself be treated as experiential absence.
6.6 Conflicting evidence
Unresolved evidence conflict routes the case to INDETERMINATE unless an independently registered precedence rule has been established. The present machine contract does not permit a desired conclusion to break the tie.
6.7 Missing evidence is not NEGATIVE evidence
The Consciousness contract source explicitly enumerates null or missing-signal patterns that are insufficient by themselves for NEGATIVE:
no report;
no movement;
no command following;
failed covert task;
low behavioral score;
no recalled content;
proxy non-detection.
A dedicated negative route is required. Missing positive evidence does not become negative experiential evidence.
6.8 Capacity evidence is not current-mode evidence
A proxy validated only for capacity cannot classify the current mode by itself. Such a case is routed to INDETERMINATE if that is the only basis for the mode claim.
6.9 What POSITIVE and NEGATIVE admission require
For a POSITIVE case, the contract requires adequate pinning, candidate independence, temporal adequacy, no unresolved conflict, and at least one affirmative route among first-person evidence, affirmative report, validated positive proxy, or convergent positive bundle. A POSITIVE admission then warrants the domain-owned experiential predicate.
For a NEGATIVE case, the contract requires adequate pinning, candidate independence, temporal adequacy, no unresolved conflict, a dedicated negative probe, and memory-confound clearance where the mode claim is retrospective. A NEGATIVE admission then warrants absence of the domain-owned experiential predicate.
The machine does not manufacture the domain experiential predicate. The domain owns the predicate; the contract governs lawful admission to or exclusion from it.

12. FROM THE FORMAL STOP TO THE EXPERIMENT
The machine stop identifies one exact missing empirical entry burden:
required NEGATIVE experiential anchor = OPEN.
The primary scientific artifact carries a staged empirical program designed to attempt to answer that burden without weakening the admission contract.
12.1 Stage A — negative-instrument / memory-confound validation
Stage A asks whether the A/B/C/D/E experiential instrument falsely classifies independently known-positive experience as complete experiential absence when later memory or content access is genuinely challenged.
The primary quantity is:
q_D = P(category D | prior positive experience independently established).
The design gate uses 93 independent index validation cases and requires a one-sided 95% exact Clopper-Pearson upper bound below 0.05. At N = 93, zero or one false-D case can satisfy the gate; two or more fail it.
A Stage-A PASS would permit the dedicated negative datum to enter the target branch under the frozen design. It would not itself create a C3R NEGATIVE anchor.
12.2 Stage B — repeated prospectively bounded extended-cessation target observation
Stage B applies the validated instrument to prospectively bounded extended-cessation attempts in qualified participants.
The target geometry is:
PRE-POSITIVE
→ entry transition
→ boundary-protected adjudication core
→ exit transition
→ POST-POSITIVE.
The protected core alone is eligible for whole-passage adjudication. Entry and exit uncertainty are excluded from the core rather than repaired retrospectively.
A candidate NEGATIVE requires, among the frozen burdens:
- whole-core category-D judgment;
- demonstrated distinction from content loss, minimal presence, and uncertainty;
- memory/access guard PASS;
- no unresolved positive contradiction;
- candidate-independent classification;
- protocol integrity;
- capacity continuity.
12.3 Stage C — locked adjudication
After Stage-B data lock, Stage C applies the preregistered decision rule to frozen evidence. It may not collect new primary target data, move boundaries, reword the experiential categories, change the memory guard, add post hoc biomarker thresholds, or repair an inconvenient result.
Only evidence that clears the frozen NEGATIVE admission route can discharge the presently OPEN negative-anchor burden.
12.4 How the formal and empirical pieces connect
The relation between formal stop and experiment is:
missing NEGATIVE anchor
→ OPEN required domain-entry burden
→ Stage A validates the negative instrument
→ if PASS, Stage B observes prospectively bounded target passages
→ Stage C applies the locked admission rule
→ a lawfully admitted NEGATIVE passage can alter the empirical ledger
→ the formal packet may then be rerun against the changed domain state.
The Lean release does not encode the full experimental protocol, sample design, or statistical analysis. The experiment remains a domain-owned empirical program linked to the exact burden identified by the formal stop.

17. CONCLUSION
The formal result is deliberately bounded. C3R is structurally located at the declared passage-specific scope, and the Experiential Admission Contract makes the evidentiary burden explicit. The proposed experiment addresses precisely this open empirical burden. Stage A tests whether the negative instrument survives a memory/access challenge in independently known-positive cases. If it does, Stage B applies that instrument to prospectively bounded target passages, and Stage C applies the frozen adjudication rule to locked evidence. Only a lawfully admitted NEGATIVE passage can change the present evidentiary standing relevant to this checkpoint.
The Lean result is therefore an auditable stop condition, not a demonstration of experiential absence and not a claim to have solved Consciousness.
